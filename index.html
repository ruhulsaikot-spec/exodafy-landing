How it works

From expense to payment
in four steps.

No manual follow-ups. No lost receipts. No spreadsheets to reconcile.

01
📝
Employee submits a claim

Via web, mobile app, or WhatsApp. Attach receipts, enter expense details, and submit — online or offline.

02
🔀
Workflow routes automatically

Claims are matched to the right approval workflow — by expense type, amount range, or department.

03
✅
Approvers act in one tap

Line manager, Finance, HR — each approver gets notified and can approve, reject, or return with comments.

04
💸
Finance verifies and pays

Finance reviews the verified amount, processes payment, and the employee is notified instantly.
